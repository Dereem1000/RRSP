Everything in One Portal

RRMS brings repair intake, bench work, parts, billing, and checkout together in one branded online portal. Pick the shop modules you need — your menu stays focused, with no clutter from areas you do not use.

Key selling point

Shop Parts & Marketplace

More than inventory on a spreadsheet — list stock, buy from other shops and technicians, and complete pickup or delivery with quotes, payment, and history built in.

Track your inventory

Add parts to My Stock with part numbers, brands, quantities, and prices. Control how much is available to sell versus held for bench work.

Sell from your stock

List available quantity so other technicians and repair companies can request your parts. Manage Incoming orders and get paid when buyers complete payment.

Buy what you need

Browse the Marketplace — compare suppliers, prices, and distance. Track requests under Outgoing through delivery or pickup.

Shop Orders

Orders

  • Optional cost fields for job materials
  • Tied to specific repair work
4

Shop Parts & Marketplace

Marketplace

The Shop Parts module combines private inventory with a shared marketplace. You are not limited to your own shelf — you can source parts from other shops when a job is urgent, and monetize surplus stock by listing it for others to buy.

  • My Stock — create and edit listings (name, part number, brand, price, quantity, available to sell)
  • Marketplace — search catalog items from other sellers; see multiple suppliers, price range, and distance
  • Outgoing — track your buy requests from pending through delivery or pickup
  • Incoming — fulfill orders when someone buys from your listed stock
  • History — completed requests, routes, and billing status
  • Quotes and online payment for delivery packages; cash-on-delivery option where supported
  • Add received parts to your stock after delivery; sell marketplace-listed parts at the counter via Shop POS
